How to read this
  • H2H is the real regular-season record. Median is a win for every week your score beats the league median that week (the top half of the league), a loss otherwise. Total adds the two.
  • Avg finish is where your score ranked among all teams each week, averaged (1 is the week's high score).
  • Expected H2H wins is what you would have won against an average schedule: each week, the share of other teams you outscored.
  • Luck is actual H2H wins minus expected H2H wins. Positive means the schedule helped you.
  • PA vs avg is points against minus the league's average points against. Negative means opponents scored less against you than they did against a typical team.
  • Regular season only; playoffs are excluded. Teams are ranked by total wins, then H2H wins, then points for.
