Welcome to the 13th edition of the LWFFL

A reminder of the rules as we head into another illustrious season.

September 16, 2026· LWFFL News & Notes

Here’s a quick reminder of the league rules:

Lineups: Start 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 WR/TE, 1 FLEX, 1 D/ST and 1 K. Players lock when their games begin, so monitor injuries and kickoff times.

IR: We have two IR spots. Players designated as out are eligible. Leaving a healthy player on IR will prevent further roster moves.

Waivers: Everyone begins with $100 in FAAB. Bids process early Wednesday morning, $0 bids are allowed, and tied bids go to the lower-ranked team.

Free agency: Unclaimed players become first-come, first-served after waivers and remain available until their games begin.

Trades: Accepted trades process immediately. The deadline is noon ET on the Wednesday before Week 11. This year, that's Wednesday, Nov. 18 at noon.

Regular season: We play 14 matchups. Each team faces its three divisional opponents twice and every non-divisional opponent once.

Playoffs: Six teams qualify. The three division winners receive seeds 1–3, the best remaining record earns seed 4, and the final two spots go to the highest-scoring remaining teams.

Seeding: The top two seeds receive first-round byes. The No. 3 seed chooses its first-round opponent, and the No. 1 seed chooses its semifinal opponent.

Payouts: $450 for the champion, $175 for second, $100 for third and $75 for fourth. The regular-season points leader also receives $100.

Good luck.
