The Notebook has a home

Previews, Sunday updates and recaps now live on the site instead of scrolling away in chat — with the numbers wired straight into the league's own box scores.

September 16, 2026· Cobey

Every week of the Notebook has, until now, existed as a wall of text in the group chat that slid out of view by Wednesday. That stops here.

What changes

Each edition gets its own page and its own link. Paste that link in chat and it unfurls with the week, the headline and a card — so people know whether they're clicking a recap or a rule change before they tap.

The write-ups themselves don't change. Same three editions, same voice:

  • Preview lands Thursday, with the lines and the matchups worth watching.
  • Sunday Update goes out in the morning, once the Thursday points are banked.
  • Recap arrives Tuesday, after everything is final.

What's actually new

The numbers in a recap are no longer typed in by hand. They're pulled live from the same box scores that feed Records and Head-to-Head, which means two things: a score on this page can never disagree with a score anywhere else on the site, and a recap from 2026 will still be correct when you read it in 2031.

Anything in the record book gets flagged automatically. If someone drops a top-25 score, the post says so without me noticing first.

Announcements live here too

Rule changes, dues, draft night, trade deadline — anything that shouldn't scroll away gets pinned at the top of the Notebook instead of being relitigated in chat every October.

If it matters in December, it goes on this page. If it's trash talk, it stays in the chat where it belongs.
