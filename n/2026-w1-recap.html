Week 1 Recap: Stuart's RBs power him to high score; Adam struggles

Runner-up from last year makes a statement in the opening week

September 16, 2026· LWFFL News & Notes

Headlines:

  • Stuart beat Connor 138.30–104.36. Swift, Montgomery and Bijan Robinson combined for 83 points. Connor’s score would have won three other matchups.
  • Thomas edged Will 90.22–81.96. Will left Jalen Coker’s 27.40 on the bench; starting him over A.J. Brown would have changed the winner.
  • Cobey posted the week’s second-highest score (123.72). Adam posted the lowest (63.22).

Odds & Ends:

  • Stuart’s 138.30 ranks third among his career scores. Connor won their first two meetings; Stuart has won the last three.
  • Thomas now leads Will 9–4. Their 2023 meeting was decided by 0.22 points.
  • Matt’s win over Adam was nearly a rerun. Their previous meeting ended 88.02–61.90; this one ended 89.96–63.22.
  • Adam scored even less in last year’s opener—60.54—and somehow won.
  • Cobey’s 42-point win over Ryan T. was the largest margin in their 12 meetings.
  • Samuel has beaten Bryce five straight times, though Bryce still leads the series 7–6. Chad ended a three-game slide against Ryan C., who still leads their series 10–4.
