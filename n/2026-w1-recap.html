Recap · Week 1

Sundseth opens at 138 and nobody else gets close

Week 1 delivered one blowout, one eight-point thriller, and the worst opening day of Adam Goho's career. Six games, and only one of them was ever in doubt.

September 16, 2026· Cobey Culton

The first Sunday of a fantasy season is supposed to tell you nothing. This one told us at least one thing: Stuart Sundseth is not rebuilding.

The big number

138.30. That's what Sundseth hung on Connor Glair, who put up 104.36 — a score that would have won four of the other five games this week and still lost by 34. There is no lineup decision available to Connor that changes the outcome. Some weeks you simply run into it.

The only real game

Tom Hos over Will Shoe by 8.26 was the week's lone contest, and the only matchup where either manager had a reason to watch the late window. Everything else was settled by early afternoon.

Elsewhere

Cobey Culton's 123.72 was the second-best score of the week and buried Ryan Turnau by 42. Samuel Wheeler and Matt Dengler both cleared 89 against opponents who didn't reach 75.

Which brings us to the other end.

Adam Goho, 63.22

The lowest opening-week score of his twelve seasons. He was never in it, and against a Dengler team that scored 89.96 — respectable, not fearsome — it didn't take much. One week is one week. But it's a hole.

What it means

Nothing, probably. Week 1 correlates with nothing except the standings, and the standings in September are decoration. Check back in five weeks and the league will look different.

Except for Sundseth. That looked real.
