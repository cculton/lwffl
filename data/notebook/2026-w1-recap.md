**Headlines:**

- [Stuart beat Connor 138.30–104.36](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2026&w=1&m=5). Swift, Montgomery and Bijan Robinson combined for 83 points. Connor’s score would have won three other matchups.
- [Thomas edged Will 90.22–81.96](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2026&w=1&m=1). Will left Jalen Coker’s 27.40 on the bench; starting him over A.J. Brown would have changed the winner.
- Cobey posted the week’s second-highest score ([123.72](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2026&w=1&mgr=cobey)). Adam posted the lowest ([63.22](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2026&w=1&mgr=adam)).

**Odds & Ends:**

- Stuart’s 138.30 ranks [third among his career scores](https://lwffl.com/records.html). Connor won their first two meetings; [Stuart has won the last three](https://lwffl.com/h2h.html?a=stuart&b=connor).
- Thomas now leads Will [9–4](https://lwffl.com/h2h.html?a=tom&b=will). Their [2023 meeting](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2023&w=7&mgr=tom) was decided by **0.22 points**.
- Matt’s win over Adam was nearly a rerun. Their [previous meeting](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2025&w=11) ended 88.02–61.90; [this one](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2026&w=1&mgr=matt) ended 89.96–63.22.
- Adam scored even less in [last year’s opener](https://lwffl.com/boxscores.html?y=2025&w=1)—60.54—and somehow won.
- Cobey’s 42-point win over Ryan T. was the [largest margin in their 12 meetings](https://lwffl.com/h2h.html?a=cobey&b=ryan-t).
- Samuel has beaten Bryce **five straight times**, though [Bryce still leads the series 7–6](https://lwffl.com/h2h.html?a=samuel&b=bryce). Chad ended a three-game slide against Ryan C., who [still leads their series 10–4](https://lwffl.com/h2h.html?a=chad&b=ryan-c).
